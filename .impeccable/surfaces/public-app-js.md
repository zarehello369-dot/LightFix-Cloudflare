## Direction contract

**THESIS:** One coordination centre for a numbered streetlight. Every role sees the same visual grammar for a pole, its status, and the next action; the public form stays the quickest path to submit a report.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep teal command header, pale green-gray working ground, white records, precise yellow actions and outline bulb. Role labels and textual statuses identify context without changing brand. Use a distinctive self-hosted Latin wordmark face for “LightFix”, separate from the bulb; Thai interface text remains highly readable.

**STORY:** A resident enters pole and symptom, submits, and gets a trackable link. A technician opens current work, reads evidence, and records repair. An administrator sees unique pole queues and assigns from each pole. The same record, control, and status shapes connect the journeys.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** At phone width, dark compact header, direct title, short instruction, and the task itself begin immediately. Public: pole input then symptom. Technician: current work count then first actionable pole. Admin: waiting count then first pole requiring assignment. The yellow primary action sits inside the relevant form or record. At tablet width, two columns; desktop adds a persistent work rail and contextual detail without changing hierarchy.
